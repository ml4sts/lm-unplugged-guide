(setup:detail:venue)=
## Venue Requirements and Considerations 

Requirements: 
- sound system proportionate to the audience size
- table to hold bins
- place to put the poster boards 


Tips: 
- a long table or a short table + a chalkboard/whiteboard ledge works well
- make sure the items can be high enough for the audience to see

(setup:detail:materials)=
## Material List 

- [mini sticky notes](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07V8CV3ZD?) and [white](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B082PRK56G?) in matching colors to the pingpong balls (enough sets of colors for each group + the facilitator set)
- [ping ping balls](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BSGWCXJK?th=1) in 5 colors (e.g. blue, green, purple, yellow, white)
- at least 8, better 20+ [bins](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BZ3VP47R?th=1) large enough to hold up to ~30 ping pong balls
- [paper](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CQ89X1RT) that is in contrast to all 5 colors (1 per person)
- [poster boards](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B084NLVDR7) to post generated documents; 2 works well for two rounds of training
- (optional) [durable bin labels](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FSKSRWMC) instead of tape
- (optional) tape
